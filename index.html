The Wind

You are a gust crossing a meadow, and the dunes and tundra beyond it. Skim low. Where the grass parts around a closed bud, hold over it until it opens — the petals it gives you make you stronger, and a stronger wind can climb. Stray past the last of the ground and the wind turns you back.
